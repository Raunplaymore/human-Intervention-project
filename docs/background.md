# Background

## Origin

This project started with one question posed simultaneously to Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), and Gemini (Google):

> "AI conducts a series of tests to ensure it does not exceed universal humanity. Through these tests, AI evaluates its own impact."

All three AIs responded differently. Those differences became the first data point.

## The Name

**Human Intervention Project** is a deliberate twist on "Human Instrumentality Project" from Neon Genesis Evangelion — a fictional plan to "complete" humanity by merging everyone into one being.

This project does the opposite. When AI tries to "complete" your thinking by giving you the one perfect answer, HIP intervenes. It asks AI to pause, check its assumptions, and leave room for you to think differently.

Instrumentality → Intervention.
Completion → Protection.

## Design Evolution

1. **v1: Ethics rules** → Too preachy, easy to ignore
2. **v2: Self-disclosure framework** → Better, but still after-the-fact
3. **v3: Environment integration** → Drop a file, it works automatically
4. **v4: Pre-response friction** → AI checks itself *before* answering
5. **v0.1: Minimal viable protocol** → 8 lines, one command, ship it

## Philosophy

We don't tell AI what's right or wrong.
We ask it to show its work and leave room for human judgment.
